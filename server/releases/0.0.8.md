# LocalGhost wisp 0.0.8

Cut on 12 November 2026. The eighth cut of wisp; the name is the release line, the number the
cut. Everything in [0.0.1's notes](0.0.1.md) still holds, and the pages for [0.0.2](0.0.2.md),
[0.0.3](0.0.3.md), [0.0.4](0.0.4.md), [0.0.5](0.0.5.md), [0.0.6](0.0.6.md) and
[0.0.7](0.0.7.md) say what each cut changed.

A box on 0.0.7 that takes 0.0.8 gains two columns and a table at its first unlock (the schema
converges by itself) and, in the first pass ten minutes later, folds every month that ended six
weeks ago or more: MEMORIES shows a month where it showed that month's days, and the days are
one tap down. The links between memories are drawn in the same pass, and the first chat of each
day chooses the front page. Over the days that follow, a "?" appears beside any memory the box
read again and could not find in the record it came from. Clips get their running time when
ghost.framed starts. `redeploy.sh` installs the box's firewall table, which existed only as
typed commands before; `sudo ./tools/server_setup_root.sh`, run once more, narrows the sudo
rule and turns security updates on; `sudo ./tools/privacy_check.sh` should then show both model
servers refusing a request that comes without their key. A box with backups switched on should
run `ghost-cli ghost.framed backup full=true` once: no photo original was in a backup before
this cut. A phone on 0.0.7 keeps its pairing, and moves its web search key into the phone's
encrypted store the first time it is read. The relay's own changes are in the web repository
(`deploy/mail`): `root-once.sh` once more for the new certificate hook, then `install.sh`.

## What changed since 0.0.7

- Mail reads as paragraphs. The first message through the relay (24 October, Gmail to the box,
  the first round trip) reached the phone as three-word tails: Gmail breaks plain text at 76
  columns and does not say so. The box now honours `format=flowed` where a sender declares it
  (RFC 3676, DelSp too) and, where none is declared, joins the lines a wrapper broke, by the
  wrapper's own rule read backwards (a line was broken before a word only if the word did not
  fit the widest line), leaving lists, indented text, blank lines, quotes at another depth and
  the signature separator alone; runs of blank lines stop at two. Messages already on the box
  keep the text they were stored with; the sealed original is untouched either way.
- The app's buttons keep their label on one line. Three in a row (ARCHIVE, UNREAD, DELETE under a
  message) used to break the word; the label shrinks a step instead and the button's side
  padding is a third of the Material default.
- Mail reaches the phone seconds after it is delivered, not minutes. Three waits were stacked:
  the relay looked at its queue every half minute, the box asked the relay every two minutes, and
  SENT reloaded only when opened. The relay now delivers the moment a box posts, the box asks
  again at 4, 10, 25 and 60 seconds after a send, and SENT reloads every four seconds while a
  row still says "waiting at the relay".
- The relay sends from its IPv4 address. Its first message to Gmail went from the box's IPv6,
  which the SPF record does not name, and failed SPF; the relay now dials receivers over IPv4
  first and IPv6 only for a host with no IPv4 address.
- An OpenPGP implementation of the box's own, `internal/pgp`, for end-to-end encrypted mail
  (the next cut wires it into MAIL): version 4 keys, Ed25519 and Curve25519 for the box, RSA
  and the NIST curves accepted from others, AES-256 in the integrity-protected packet, SHA-256
  signatures, the three compressions on read, armor. Built on the standard library's primitives
  alone, 1,900 lines, tested against GnuPG 2.4 both ways (its keys and ours, each compression,
  signing subkeys, a NIST key, an expired key). Nothing in MAIL changes yet.
- `ghost-ctl doctor`: the box's own check-up, one screen of PASS/WARN/FAIL with the fix on the
  next line. Every port open to the network and the process and user holding it, with anything
  of the box's on a public address other than 80, 443 and the relay's 25 a FAIL (secd's plain
  port, the vault's databases, a node's RPC, the model server, code-server); the firewall
  tables; nginx and the edge's two shapes agreeing with `/etc/ghost/edge`; certificate expiry,
  the box's and Let's Encrypt's; ssh keys-only and no root; unattended security updates and a
  pending reboot; every mail DNS record the box needs looked up, and the relay's PTR and
  greeting; the box's name answering on 443. It changes nothing (tools/README.md 8e).
- The firewall table is part of the tree. `tools/nft/localghost.nft` keeps secd's plain
  loopback port to root and nginx (the audit's forged-header hole, closed in the kernel);
  `tools/firewall.sh` installs it under `/etc/nftables.d` with `/etc/nftables.conf` including
  the directory, so it survives a reboot, and `server_setup_root.sh` and `redeploy.sh` run it.
  The relay's own table (port 25 limited per source, nothing on IPv6) is installed the same way
  by its `root-once.sh` in the web repo. Both are tables of their own; the machine's other rules
  are not touched. Until now these rules existed only as commands typed on one box.
- `server_setup_root.sh` installs nftables and unattended-upgrades and turns security updates
  on; `redeploy.sh` no longer re-renders the ghost-secd nginx site on a box with the stream
  passthrough (that put a second listen 443 beside the stream's); `ghost-setup` makes the
  daemons' user by the name given (`--user`), not a `ghost` user it never used.
- GALLERY refreshed. A video is told from a photo at a glance: its cell wears a green frame with
  ▶ and the running time at the foot (the box now reads a clip's duration from its header and
  fills older rows in at start), and a "[ ▶ videos ]" switch shows the clips alone. The frame's
  card is new: the picture at its own shape rather than a square, "> VIDEO · 1:42" or "> PHOTO"
  as the heading, the name, when and how big, where, what the box saw, and the tags as chips in
  their groups (people, place, event, activity …), each with its ✕ and a line to add one. A
  result opened from the search now shows its tags, place and description too; it used to say
  "none yet" for tags it had plainly matched on. The screen stays on and the phone does not lock
  while a clip plays or a slideshow runs.
- The box reads its memories again. A memory distilled from a chat or a note was written once and
  then believed, so a misreading lived on in every later answer. Now, once a day on the GPU, a few
  per pass, synthd fetches the record a memory was made from and asks the model whether the record
  says this; YES stamps it, NO or PARTLY puts it in doubt with the record's own words beside it
  (kept only when its names and numbers are in the record). A doubted memory stays, marked "?" in
  MEMORIES with what was found on its page, out of the chat's grounding and out of a person's
  written body, until you edit, delete or leave it. Facts about people are checked one by one.
  Your own edits are never read again; nothing is deleted. `ghost-cli ghost.synthd read-again`.
- A second security audit (10 November), and what it changed. The first audit's fixes held;
  this one went through what had been built since and what had been left for later. Three
  areas were not covered that day (the mail relay, local privilege boundaries on the box, the
  media parsers); they were gone through on 12 November, further down this page.
  - Unlock. The PIN is always tried through the attempt limiter, and a second phone arriving
    during a running unlock waits for its own turn instead of being handed the first one's; a
    wrong PIN counts against the phone that typed it and ends that phone's session, not the
    owner's; behind nginx, the certificate in the header is verified against the box's device
    CA, where any non-empty header used to pass.
  - Root's writes onto the vault. ghost.secd runs as root and puts the phone's uploads into
    folders the daemons' user owns. It did so by path, so a daemon that had been taken over
    could point root's writes, and the ownership root hands over, at any directory on the
    machine. Every such write (photos, notes, voice, health, news batches, mail, refreshed
    binaries, model weights) now walks down from the volume's root without following a link
    and owns files through their descriptors.
  - Where the box connects. The box's own fetches (feeds, linked articles, coin sites, the
    weather centres) are https only, to public addresses only, checked again at the
    connection, five redirects at most, and the Met Office key never follows a redirect to
    another host. A link in a feed can no longer make the box ask its own loopback or the
    router. A page that is not UTF-8 no longer takes ghost.synthd down, and a fault in reading
    anything from outside costs one pass, not the daemon. A feed is kept to its first 300
    entries.
  - Mail. What MAIL shows as the sender is the address the checks were run on, by one reading
    of the header; an address that is not a plain one (a quoted name with spaces, built so
    that a cut shows someone else's address) is shown as "an unreadable sender" and scored.
    Names and subjects are one line with hidden direction marks removed and named. "Looks
    genuine" is said only when the From domain was proven; otherwise "no signs of spam, sender
    not proven". A signature over part of a body does not count, two strangers under an
    unlisted `com.xx` no longer align with each other, a slow SPF lookup no longer starves the
    DMARC one. A very long hard-wrapped message is read in its length, not its square (1.4 MB
    took almost five minutes), and a short last line is no longer pulled into the paragraph
    before it. Web pages and SVGs as attachments are not kept.
  - Backups. The nightly backup now holds the photo and video originals. It had been sealing
    `framed/` (the Timeline folders) when the archive is `frames/archive`: no original was ever
    in a backup. Incrementals go by when a file landed, not the photo's own date; every backup
    ends with a manifest of what the box held; `ghost.restore unseal file out.tar` writes
    nothing unless the whole file authenticated. A box with backups already switched on should
    run `ghost-cli ghost.framed backup full=true` once.
  - Deletes. Deleting a chat takes its copy out of the journal (it used to stay, and memories
    were made from it afterwards); the day's story drops the model's text when words it was
    written from are taken back, and a day with nothing left is not told; forgetting a day's
    fixes removes its path and route files; a memory you deleted is not written again when the
    note about you is re-read. Memory titles and record quotes no longer go to the INFO log.
  - The read-again pass (not yet released when audited). It no longer doubts a fact that has
    no record to be read against (every fact from a check-in was doubted without asking the
    model), reads a check-in's facts against the check-in, and a YES stands only when the
    memory's names and numbers are in the record.
  - The app. "Use on-phone models only" on the lock gate drops the box session (it opened the
    whole app on the stored two-day session with no fingerprint and no PIN); "re-scan the box
    QR" asks for the phone's unlock first; a picker or the camera left open more than two
    minutes, or the screen going dark, means the gate; text shared from another app is shown
    and asked about before it becomes a note; LOCK drops the phone's session even when the box
    did not answer, and says the box is still open; a finished clip and a slideshow no longer
    hold the screen on for ever. A photo attached to a chat is decoded and fitted on the box
    like any other before the model sees it.
  - OpenPGP (`internal/pgp`, still not wired into MAIL). A genuine signed message with other
    data attached no longer comes back as that data with the signer's name; revocations count
    wherever they stand (GnuPG writes them first); the newest subkey binding decides; critical
    subpackets the box does not know make a signature unusable; keys and messages are bounded
    (RSA to 8192 bits, signatures per key, packets, decompressed size); a key is judged again
    at the time it is used.
- The chat looks things up more readily. In auto mode the phone used to ask the box for a
  search plan only when the question held a word from one list (today, price, latest …), so
  "who built hagia sophia" was answered from what the model remembered. Now the box's model is
  asked about every question that is not small talk and not plainly about your own life; it
  is told to search when unsure, to cover two readings of an unclear question, and to name the
  subject, and it always gives three searches so a thin first round has one left to try. The
  plan no longer waits behind a photo being described (it used to time out there and the phone
  gave up on the web). The box's Wikipedia is tried by the question's own words when they are
  typed without capitals, and by what the plan says the question needs when a follow-up names
  nothing; on the phone, the plan's subject fetches Wikipedia's summary beside the search
  results. Every search is shown in the answer's trail before it leaves, and is held to a rule
  on the box and again on the phone: nothing in the first person, no address, no long number,
  and none of your people's names unless you typed the name yourself.
- The known gap "mail from a home connection refused by the large receivers until the sending IP
  has a reverse name" is softened to the spam folder: Gmail took the box's first message on the
  first try and filed it in Spam.
- "Feels like" counts the sun. The box's own forecast gave the shade figure at all hours: 3°
  and "feels -2" at noon under a clear sky. With the sun up and the cloud letting it through,
  what a person standing in it absorbs is added (Steadman's term, from the sun's height and the
  cloud cover): the same hour reads about 5°. At night and under full cloud nothing changes.
- The areas the second audit did not reach, and the app end to end (12 November):
  - The phone. A paired phone no longer takes another box's QR on sight: it stops, names both
    boxes, and pairs only on ENROL. Enrolling again starts clean (the old session's token and
    caches went to the new box before). WIPE THIS PHONE now also takes the session token,
    voice notes not yet sent, the last news, prices and weather, the map's last view and the
    search key. Links in feeds, coin pages and search results open only when they are web
    addresses. A web page built to be slow can no longer hold a core for minutes. Other apps
    cannot draw over the app, and the PIN screen is blank in a screen share. The app's network
    policy is named and allows no cleartext anywhere. The crash report shown before the gate
    says where, not what was being read. A release's notes from the mirror are held to their
    shape before they are shown. A camera-roll picture dated in the future no longer stops
    the photo sync until that date.
  - The mail relay (the web repository). One message can no longer carry a second inside it
    (the data ends on CRLF, dot, CRLF and nothing else), in either direction. A receiver
    cannot fill the relay's memory, stall everyone's outgoing mail, or write the text of the
    report the relay sends a box. Mail is delivered to public addresses only. The scripts
    that run as root no longer write by name into folders root does not own.
  - On the box. The model servers ask for a key: before, any account on the machine could
    ask the model and watch what it was working on. The update tools, which run as root on
    the unlocked volume, no longer follow a link there (two of their lines would have given a
    taken-over daemon root). ffmpeg and dwebp get the same size limits as the box's own
    decoders, a deadline, and a bounded reply; a WebP's size is read before it is decoded; a
    clip built as millions of empty boxes is put down at once; a photo dated in the year 9999
    is filed by the phone's date.
- Memories are kept the way the posts say they should be ("How memory gets made"), four steps
  closer. `docs/MEMORY.md` is the whole model, with what is built and what is not.
  - Anchors. On a memory's page, `[ ◆ mark as an anchor ]` marks it as one of the few your
    life hangs on. Only you can: the box counts what goes together, it does not decide what
    mattered, and no pass sets, lifts, deletes or folds away an anchor. Anchors come first in
    MEMORIES, have a chip of their own, and lead what every chat starts from.
  - The company a memory keeps. A memory's page lists what the box holds with it and why:
    the people it names, the place it was at, the day it was written on, the trip or the
    month it is part of. Each is a tap away. The links are drawn from the memories with no
    model, so each is something the box can point at. A deleted memory leaves every
    neighbourhood at once.
  - Months. A month that ended six weeks ago becomes one memory, counted from its days (how
    many, the photos, the places, the trips, the walking, the notes), with its days and
    outings under it the way a trip holds its days. Nothing is deleted: the days are one tap
    down and what they were told from is where it was. Recent weeks stay day by day.
  - The front page. Every chat used to start from the first 1,200 characters of the note
    about you, whatever the note went on to say. It starts now from a page chosen once a day:
    the note's opening, your anchors, and the few memories that weigh the most (the ones in
    company, lately made or lately looked at). The memories on it say so.
  - A question brings better memories. Of two that match equally the heavier goes first, not
    the newer, and the best match brings a neighbour the question did not name: ask about a
    person and the trip they were on can come with them.
  - Fixed: a question that matched nothing in the archive showed three "memories" under the
    answer with invented dates ("photo caption placeholder"). They were test scaffolding from
    the first weeks and are gone. Nothing found is nothing shown.
  - Fixed: a day told again (a late caption, a late sync) lost its place under its trip until
    the next folding and showed on its own in the feed.
  - Fixed: an outing under an old trip could open as "this memory is not on the box any
    more". The page looked for its memory in the feed, which stops at six hundred rows with
    the parts last; it asks the box for the memory by name now.

## Known gaps at 0.0.8

- Carried from 0.0.7: the box CA key on the OS disk by choice; releases signed by the site key
  alone; the APK not byte-reproducible; the Mist and the decoy volume not built; mail from a home
  connection filed in Spam by the large receivers until the sending IP has a reverse name; the box's
  forecast with no verification yet (nothing scores yesterday's forecast against what happened,
  so the blend's weights are set by hand); the mirror's daemon specified and not built.
- From the first audit's leads, still open: the calendar and contacts device label comes from
  the upload, not the certificate. (The share handler, the picker guard and the unseen web
  queries are closed in this cut.)
- From the 12 November pass, left as they are: the update tools still download by name into
  the unlocked volume as root (a daemon that had been taken over could aim an update's bytes
  at another file: destruction, not control; the lasting fix is to do that work as the
  volume's owner, and it needs the box to test); the relay's outgoing mail has no MTA-STS or
  DANE (a receiver's certificate that does not verify is still delivered to, encrypted); the
  daemons' health ports tell any local account their counts; a voice note waits on the phone
  as plain audio until the box has it.
- The memory model, against the posts (`docs/MEMORY.md` has the list): memories are matched
  by word, not by meaning, so two things alike in meaning that share no word are not linked
  and not found together; there is no queue of questions with yes, no and don't ask again;
  ghost.cued's context priming has nothing to prime against, so what comes unasked is a day a
  year ago and something nearby; there are no years above the months; a memory's kind cannot
  be changed from the phone. A person's memory keeps what you said of them as facts and the
  model writes them up, which is more than the "pointer" the overhears post describes.
- From the second audit, open by choice or waiting on a decision: the box still reads linked articles and coin
  sites itself while SETTINGS says only the phone fetches on Wi-Fi; other people's text that
  reaches a chat answer can be distilled into a memory; a shared text that starts "Daily
  check-in <date>" is filed as that day's check-in (it is now shown and asked about first);
  deleting a day's memory on MEMORIES does not stop the DAY page telling it; a backup proves
  secrecy and wholeness, not who wrote it; incrementals do not replay deletions; mail has no
  disk ceiling; the short install check cannot verify the bundle it downloads, the mirror
  script accepts an expired or revoked site-key signature, and the site key signs everything
  from the web host's keyring.
