# LocalGhost wisp 0.0.8

In progress, since 23 October 2026. The eighth cut of wisp; the name is the release line, the
number the cut. Everything in [0.0.1's notes](0.0.1.md) still holds, and the pages for
[0.0.2](0.0.2.md), [0.0.3](0.0.3.md), [0.0.4](0.0.4.md), [0.0.5](0.0.5.md), [0.0.6](0.0.6.md)
and [0.0.7](0.0.7.md) say what each cut changed.

At the cut this page gets its date line, CITATION.cff its version and date, RELEASES.md its
row, and the README its status paragraph and line counts.

## What changed since 0.0.7

- Mail reads as paragraphs. The first message through the relay (24 October, Gmail to the box,
  the first round trip) reached the phone as three-word tails: Gmail breaks plain text at 76
  columns and does not say so. The box now honours `format=flowed` where a sender declares it
  (RFC 3676, DelSp too) and, where none is declared, joins the lines a wrapper broke, by the
  wrapper's own rule read backwards (a line was broken before a word only if the word did not
  fit the widest line), leaving lists, indented text, blank lines, quotes at another depth and
  the signature separator alone; runs of blank lines stop at two. Messages already on the box
  keep the text they were stored with; the sealed original is untouched either way.
- The app's buttons keep their label on one line. Three in a row (ARCHIVE, UNREAD, DELETE under a
  message) used to break the word; the label shrinks a step instead and the button's side
  padding is a third of the Material default.
- The known gap "mail from a home connection refused by the large receivers until the sending IP
  has a reverse name" is softened to the spam folder: Gmail took the box's first message on the
  first try and filed it in Spam.

## Known gaps at 0.0.8

- Carried from 0.0.7: the box CA key on the OS disk by choice; releases signed by the site key
  alone; the APK not byte-reproducible; the Mist and the decoy volume not built; mail from a home
  connection filed in Spam by the large receivers until the sending IP has a reverse name; the box's
  own forecast with no run through a real centre yet; the mirror's daemon specified and not
  built.
- From the security audit's leads: the app's share handler files a note without showing it, the
  picker guard keeps the shell unlocked in the background, planned web queries are not shown
  before they run; the calendar and contacts device label comes from the upload, not the
  certificate.
