# LocalGhost wisp 0.0.7

In progress, since 10 October 2026. The seventh cut of wisp; the name is the release line, the
number the cut. Everything in [0.0.1's notes](0.0.1.md) still holds, and the pages for
[0.0.2](0.0.2.md), [0.0.3](0.0.3.md), [0.0.4](0.0.4.md), [0.0.5](0.0.5.md) and
[0.0.6](0.0.6.md) say what each cut changed.

At the cut this page gets its date line, CITATION.cff its version and date, RELEASES.md its
row, and the README its status paragraph and line counts.

## What changed since 0.0.6

### A check-in for the day just ended

At one in the morning the day you want to tell is the one that just ended, not the one the
clock has started. The check-in page opens on yesterday in the small hours while yesterday is
still empty, and has yesterday and today as two switches under the heading at any hour; an
empty day on the two-week strip, tapped, becomes the day the form fills in, with the box's
guesses and the why prefilled from that day. The box files a check-in under the day its title
names: one written on the 10th for the 9th is stamped at the 9th's noon, so the day's story,
which reads the journal by the clock, tells the feeling under the right day; a check-in for the
day it is written on keeps its real time.

### The widget fits its cell

The widget's phrase, its sound and its meaning grow with the height the launcher gives it,
held back by its width so no line runs out of the cell, up to about one and a half times; the
dim lines under them (the head, the prices, what comes next, the buttons) grow by a quarter of
that at most, since the long ones run out of width first. The sizes chosen on the widget
(small, medium, large) are the size at two rows.

### The map stays cool

The map is drawn into a composited layer, a texture kept between frames, so the YOU marker's
breathing, the drawer sliding over the map and a line changing under it replay the texture
rather than re-render the world's paths, the tiles and the roads; only the camera, a landed tile
or the data draw them again. The marker breathes a few times when the map opens or the fix
moves, then holds still: an animation that never ended kept the window rendering at the
display's rate for as long as the map was open, with everything under it, and the phone got
hot and the drawer stuttered.

### Every trip stays on the feed

The MEMORIES feed was the newest two hundred rows by the clock, and a trip from June fell off
it once two hundred newer days and outings had been written: it was there, then it was gone.
The feed now lists whole memories first (every trip, person and fact, newest first) and the
parts folded under a trip after them, six hundred rows by default, and a trip's page asks the
box for its outings and days by the trip itself rather than looking for them in the feed.

### The top of MEMORIES

One row of chips that wrap: add a memory, jot a note, about me, what you photograph, near you,
on this day; the lit one is open and its card sits under the row. The line that pointed at the
check-in is gone (CHECK-IN is in the menu).

### A trip of one outing, and a trip that dissolves says why

A week in one town is one outing, and until now it took two outings to make a trip, so a trip
spent in one place never showed as one. A lone outing away for a night or more is a trip now
(its days fold under it as before). When a trip dissolves (its outings re-clustered, no longer
away, or gone) synthd logs the trip and what became of each outing, so a trip that vanishes
from MEMORIES can be traced in the log.

### The weather and the maps say the truth

Two cards lied. The maps card counted tiles in a folder that never held them (geo/landtiles
rather than landtiles, and the wrong suffix), so a box with the coast and the roads cut said
"no map data on the box". The weather card read tallyd's "no places" flag at the wrong level of
its answer, so a box whose place list was empty said "not pulled yet" for ever. Both read the
right thing now; the weather card also says when the pull fails (the last error, how many
batches) and when the place list cannot be read.

The place list is built from the box's GeoNames rows with a population, and the population
column came on 4 October: a set imported before it has every row at 0 and gives the weather
nothing to pull. ghost.framed now notices such a set at start and imports the same files again
in the background to fill the column; `ghost-cli ghost.framed geo-import` does the same by hand.

### The screen's stray line

The sweep that ran down the page on some page changes is gone. In its place a stray scanline
blinks somewhere in the middle three fifths of the glass for a blink or two, like interference
caught, and is gone within half a second; never at an edge, where it read as a border, and still
only now and then (CrtMood's roll, the same quiet).

### The menu, and PERMISSIONS

INTEGRATIONS is one row under THE BOX (its cards open the pages; the three sub-rows and the
doubled label are gone). PERMISSIONS joins SETTINGS below the divider: every permission the
phone asks the OS for, in one list with the reason next to each and where it stands, GRANT ALL
running the same chain as the welcome (one dialog after another in the order the OS allows),
OPEN APP SETTINGS for a line the OS will not ask about again, Health Connect's own sheet, and
the battery setting that quietly stops the trail and the sync when the app is "optimised".

### The recorder, thumb-sized, with a pause

The voice recorder's buttons are the size of a thumb: record, then stop and pause side by
side, the clock and the level readable from arm's length; a paused note keeps the microphone
open and the clock still, and resume carries on in the same take. The past check-ins list shows
one row a day (a day checked in twice shows the row with a feeling), and the day column fits
"Wed 30 Sep".

### The stock take

A pass over both sides for waste, the worst of it fixed here:

- The database client sent a statement again after a thirty-second timeout, as if the socket
  had died; a slow statement ran twice and a slow index build never finished. A statement that
  went out and was not answered is never resent now, and index builds over millions of rows
  (Wikipedia's) wait as long as they take.
- The frame-to-original lookup matched on a substring of the hash, which no index serves, so
  the stock-take of a big archive grew with the square of its size; it is a range on the index.
- A memory lookup by kind and ref had no index; every pass did one per outing, trip and day.
- The outings pass re-read every photo and tag every half hour because its change signature
  counted the trail's points (one more every quarter hour); it now watches the trail only up to
  two days ago.
- Thumbnails and previews, named by hash, go out immutable with an ETag, so a scroll back costs
  no request; POST bodies are capped (the unlock's PIN body was not).
- The maps card counted a million road tiles on every poll; once a minute now.
- On the phone: the lock-screen card and the widget parsed the phrase packs on the main thread
  at every alarm (an ANR at boot); they have a thread of their own. The notifications poll ran
  every ten seconds on the gate and the PIN screen, where there is no session; it runs in the
  shell only, every half minute. A sync rebuilt its notification for every chunk uploaded; twice
  a second now. The YOU marker's pulse restarted with every fix; it restarts when the fix moves
  thirty metres. The recorder's level no longer recomposes the whole check-in form ten times a
  second. The unlock's tickers stop when the unlock is over. The memories filter runs once per
  change, not per keystroke's recomposition.

### Smaller

- DAY says when the box did not answer, for the story and the photos, instead of "no story
  yet" and "none from this day".
- Dates read the British way everywhere the app prints one (10 Oct 2026, not Oct 10, 2026);
  "day(s)" and "frame(s)" are a word each.

## The heights, on the box

The mirror carries the Copernicus DEM at 90 m for the whole world in 69 packs of a 30-degree
block each, about 90 GB. A box takes them with

    sudo GHOST_GEO_ELEVATION=all ./tools/update.sh maps

or a region, `sudo GHOST_GEO_ELEVATION="34:72,-25:45" ./tools/update.sh maps` (latitudes 34 to
72, longitudes 25 west to 45 east: Europe; several boxes space-separated). Every file's hash
is checked against the signed manifest; a run that stops resumes where it was. Packs already on
the box are kept current by any later `update.sh maps`, asked or not. ghost.framed indexes new
packs within ten minutes and draws every day's path again with its climb and its highest point;
`sudo ./tools/ns.sh ./bin/ghost-cli ghost.framed elevation` says how many packs are in and,
with `lat= lon=`, the height at a point. The phone shows the heights on MAP (a day's trail
panel) and in the DAY story; INTEGRATIONS › Maps and heights says "heights N packs".

## Known gaps at 0.0.7

- A fresh box trusts the device-certificate header nginx passes over 127.0.0.1 until the operator
  runs `ghost-ctl edge-passthrough --domain <box name>`; a box reached by IP alone cannot take the
  passthrough yet.
- The box CA key stays on the OS disk, by choice.
- A phone left for two weeks has to be enrolled again with a fresh QR; carrying its data over
  to the new enrolment is next.
- Releases are signed by the site key alone; an offline release key is planned.
- The APK is not byte-reproducible; the server set is.
- CRYPTO50's day value against its live value showed a fault on 3 October whose cause is still
  to be named.
- Mail, the Mist (peer-to-peer backup) and the decoy volume are not built.
