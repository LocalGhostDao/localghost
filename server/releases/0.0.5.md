# LocalGhost wisp 0.0.5

In progress, since 4 October 2026. The fifth cut of wisp; the name is the release line, the
number the cut. Everything in [0.0.1's notes](0.0.1.md) still holds, and the pages for
[0.0.2](0.0.2.md), [0.0.3](0.0.3.md) and [0.0.4](0.0.4.md) say what each cut changed. This page
is written as the work lands and becomes the release's notes at the cut.

At the cut: the date in the first line above and the "In progress" with it; `version` and
`date-released` in CITATION.cff; the row in RELEASES.md; the README's status line (the server
and app line counts too). release.names and the app's version are already at 0.0.5.

## What changed since 0.0.4

### Wikipedia opens for real

0.0.4 shipped a reader that had only ever read files written to its own specification, and the
first published Wikipedia file (the June 2026 English edition without pictures, 52.7 GB,
19.7 million entries) stopped it: the file keeps no title list in its header, the title order is
an entry of its own, and that entry sits in an uncompressed cluster 1.4 GB long shared with the
search indexes, which the reader loaded whole under a 128 MB bound. The reader now reads an
uncompressed cluster's offsets and the one blob it wants in place, the title order four bytes at
a time where it lies, and keeps the whole-cluster bound for compressed clusters only (a megabyte
or two each in these files). `ghost-cli ghost.synthd wiki` names the file and its entry count.

### Wikipedia keeps its place in an answer

"Tell me about Greenwich" came back from the memories alone, and asked whether it had Wikipedia
the box said no. Two reasons, both gone. The chat gathers at most six pieces of context and asked
the memories first, so six memories filled the quota before the article had its turn; the
article's lead keeps a place of its own now. And "Greenwich london" is no title: a place said
with its city or country is looked up as "Greenwich, London", "Greenwich (London)" and plain
"Greenwich" when the last word is a qualifier (a city, a country, "park"). The model is also told
what the box holds besides the archive, the Wikipedia copy by name and date, the weather, the
prices and the news, and that it has no internet of its own, so it neither denies having
Wikipedia nor claims the web.

### The box writes the way the site writes

The chat answers in the site's voice (the web repo's writing guidelines): plain and to the point,
flat where sure and hedged where not, contractions, no bullet points, headings or bold unless a
list was asked for, no colons inside sentences, none of the words the guidelines ban, no praise
of the question, no summary at the end, no verdict, no "hope this helps". The rules ride with
every question, and the one thing a model of this size will not obey however it is told, the em
dash, is put right on the way out: em dashes become commas in the stream, token by token, and in
every stored answer, day story, memory and news summary.

### The check-in has a page of its own

The daily check-in sat in the middle of MEMORIES, between the note about me and the voice notes,
with its history a list of lines behind a toggle. It is CHECK-IN now, a page in the menu under YOUR
ARCHIVE: the day; the last two weeks as a strip, one cell a day, each marked by the quadrant the
day was told by (▲ bright, ● easy, ◆ tense, ▼ heavy, ◇ mind, a dot for nothing), a tap on a cell
opening that day's check-in; then today's, the feelings laid out as the mood meter's four
quadrants with the mind row under them, the box's guesses already ticked and marked, the why
prefilled from the day, a voice note, the save. Once saved the page is what was said, the day as
the box tells it, and a recorder for more. Under that the past check-ins as rows, what recurred
this month, and every voice note on the box. Nothing about the storage moved (the check-in is
still a journal entry, synthd distils it with the rest), and nothing counts missed days: an empty
day is a dot, and the box asks once in the evening and is silent after, as before. The evening
reminder opens this page.

### A memory has a page of its own, and a notification opens the thing

A notification that brought a memory back ("a year ago today you ...") used to land on MEMORIES
with the list filtered to that one title, chips and chrome and all. It opens the memory alone now:
what kind of thing it is, the title, its photos, the body in full, where it came from and when, the
day it was made from (a day's or an outing's memory names it, and the box sends that ref since this
cut), and edit and delete. A memory's title in the list opens the same page; a day's outing card
does too. The box's reminder for the check-in opens CHECK-IN; a nearby one opens NEAR YOU as before.

### A box from the release alone

The release bundle used to carry the daemons and the two command-line tools, and a new box still
needed git, Go and make to be set up. Since this cut the bundle carries ghost-setup, ghost-qr,
ghost-update-guard, the tile cutters and the TPM repair tool, and the operator scripts with their
pins, so a box is set up from the bundle: verify the sums against the site key, unpack,
`sudo localghost/tools/install.sh`. The installer puts the release under
`/opt/localghost/release/<version>/`, the scripts under `/opt/localghost/tools/` and the
command-line tools under `/opt/localghost/bin/` (where a release taken from the phone puts them),
and runs `setup.sh` from the release, which sees the binaries and builds nothing. The engine and the
models are a step of setup.sh now rather than a command to know about (`GHOST_LLAMA=0` leaves them
for `update.sh engine`), so the whole of it is: download and verify, install, scan, unlock, and
`update.sh` once unlocked for the streets and Wikipedia. The repository checkout still works the
same way, building first. The scripts are text and the build is the same, so the bundle is as
reproducible as it was.

Two things found on the way. The bundle's NOTES.md, there since the first cut, is not among the
files a box's unpacker admits at the top of a bundle, so a release taken from the phone (DEPLOY)
would have been refused by every box so far; the notes now sit beside the bundle in the set and
not in it, and a box on 0.0.4 can take this cut from the phone, the first time that is true. And a
release's setup binaries are left in the unpacked release by a DEPLOY, never staged with the cohort,
and ghost-update-guard is never replaced by a release at all, as its comment always said.

### The volume as a file on any drive

The volume is one LUKS container, and cryptsetup is as happy with a file as with a whole disk (it
attaches a loop device of its own). So a box's volume can now be a file on a drive that is mounted
at boot, allocated in full (`fallocate`, copy-on-write off on btrfs), instead of a whole empty disk:
`ghost-setup --image /mnt/data/localghost.img --size 500G`, or the same answers at setup.sh's volume
step (a path and a size instead of a device; 20G at least). The unit waits for the drive
(`RequiresMountsFor`), the stable-name lookup leaves a file alone, and secd says plainly when the
file is not there at unlock (the drive not mounted, most often) rather than searching the disks.
The seal, the PINs and the wipe are unchanged: the file is ciphertext, a copy of it is ciphertext,
and there is no key outside the seal. The host sees a file of a size and a date, as it saw a disk
before. Not a network share.

### The engine built for the card in the box

`setup_llama.sh` built for compute capability 8.9 by a number in the script, the reference box's
RTX 4070. It reads the capability from the driver now (`nvidia-smi`), one or several cards, and
says which it built for; a toolkit with no card the driver can see gets a CPU build, said loudly;
`GHOST_CUDA_ARCHS=89` names it by hand. Nothing to edit for another card.

## Known gaps at 0.0.5

- A fresh box trusts the device-certificate header nginx passes over 127.0.0.1 until the operator
  runs `ghost-ctl edge-passthrough --domain <box name>`; a box reached by IP alone cannot take the
  passthrough yet (nginx routes it by name), which is next.
- The box CA key stays on the OS disk, by choice: a phone can be enrolled and refused while the
  vault is locked.
- Device certificates last ten years; shorter ones renewed by the phone are next.
- Releases are signed by the site key alone; an offline release key is planned.
- The APK is not byte-reproducible; the server set is.
- The weather is a chat answer; a card on HOME and the lock screen is next.
- CRYPTO50's day value against its live value showed a fault on 3 October whose cause is still
  to be named (`ghost-cli ghost.tallyd rates index=1` lays the terms out); the guard holds the
  index meanwhile.
- After the first unlock the operator still runs `update.sh` by hand for the streets and
  Wikipedia; secd queuing it is next. A `.deb` is not built; the bundle and `install.sh` get a box up
  without one.
- NEAR YOU, what you photograph and on this day are still sections of MEMORIES; a page each is next.
- Mail, the Mist (peer-to-peer backup) and the decoy volume are not built.
